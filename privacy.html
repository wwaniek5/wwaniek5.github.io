Privacy policy

This Google OAuth app ("rclone") is used only by its owner, on their own computer, to access their own Google Drive.

  • No data is collected, stored or shared by this app or any server. Files move directly between Google Drive and the owner's computer.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No other users are served.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
